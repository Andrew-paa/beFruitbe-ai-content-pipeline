# Video Examples

[Русская версия](README_RU.md)

This directory contains real videos produced during the Proof-of-Concept stage of the **beFruitbe AI Content Pipeline**.

A total of **9 completed AI-assisted videos** were produced during the project.

All 9 were reviewed and accepted by the beFruitbe team.

Each case documents not only the final video but also the production workflow:

`task → concept → characters → references → prompts → generation → review → iterations → editing → final result`

## Example 01 — Tony Pineapple

[`01-tony-pineapple/`](01-tony-pineapple/)

A short-form cinematic video based on the mechanics of a recognizable press-conference edit.

The original human characters were replaced with anthropomorphic fruit characters designed for the beFruitbe content concept.

The production included:

- the main Tony Pineapple character;
- an anthropomorphic strawberry reporter;
- additional fruit characters in the press room;
- multi-angle character references;
- Kling video generation;
- separate voice generation;
- lip sync;
- final editing.

The video was published on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube and received approximately **3,000 cumulative views** across the three platforms.
