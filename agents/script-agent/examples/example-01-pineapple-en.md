## Parameters

**Format:** 9:16  
**Duration:** 18–20 seconds  
**Audience:** cold audience

**Primary message:** pineapple becomes part of the product concept before water is introduced during brewing.

---

## Hook

### Primary Version

**First shot:** extreme close-up of fresh pineapple → sharp cut to the dry product.

**On-screen text:**

> What happens to pineapple BEFORE the cup?

**Voice-over:**

> The most interesting part happens before brewing even begins.

### Alternative Hooks

- “Where does pineapple go before the water?”
- “The pineapple doesn't first appear in the cup.”

---

## Script

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sec | Pineapple close-up → dry leaf | “The most interesting part happens before brewing even begins.” | **PINEAPPLE → ? → СОКОчай** |
| 2–6 sec | Conceptual visualization: fruit-like texture moves toward the surface of the leaf | Explanation of the juice-related production concept after factual confirmation | **Juice — before water** |
| 6–10 sec | Real dry product in macro | “The flavour is therefore already part of the dry product.” | **Dry product for brewing** |
| 10–14 sec | Dry product placed into transparent brewing ware, water is added | “Water then helps reveal it during brewing.” | **Now — brewing** |
| 14–18 sec | Final drink + real beFruitbe packaging | Brand/category explanation and final identification | **СОКОчай · beFruitbe** |

---

## Key Visual Technique

The opening uses a **match cut**:

```text
pineapple texture
        ↓
dry leaf texture
```

The purpose is to visually communicate the idea without pretending to show documentary footage of the real production process.

The AI-generated sequence is treated as a conceptual visualization.

---

## AI Video Prompt

**Type:** text-to-video  
**Duration:** approximately 4 seconds

```text
Vertical 9:16 conceptual product visualization. Extreme macro of a real dried tea leaf on a clean neutral surface. A translucent natural pineapple-juice-like golden texture gently spreads across the surface of the leaf, visually suggesting juice impregnation before brewing.

Single action only: the fruit-like liquid texture gradually meets and covers the leaf.

Slow macro camera push-in, soft directional studio light, realistic organic textures, restrained premium product aesthetic.

Start with a clearly visible dry leaf, end with the same leaf surrounded by subtle juicy pineapple color and texture.

This is a conceptual visualization, not documentary factory footage.

No machinery, no cup, no pouring juice into prepared tea, no captions, no logos, no package, no fantasy transformation.
```

### Prompt Logic

The prompt explicitly defines:

- one primary action per generated shot;
- initial state;
- final state;
- camera behavior;
- lighting;
- textures;
- restrictions;
- no fictional production footage.
