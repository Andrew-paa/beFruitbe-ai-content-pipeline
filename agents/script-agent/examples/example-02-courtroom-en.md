# Example 02 — “The Pineapple Trial”

[Русская версия](example-02-courtroom-ru.md)

## Example Status

**Real request from the beFruitbe project.**

This example demonstrates a different operating mode of the Script & Prompt Agent.

Unlike Example 01, the user did not provide a specific creative concept and explicitly allowed the agent to develop the idea independently.

The main constraint was:

> create a scenario with multiple characters and a significant amount of dialogue.

From this short request, the agent independently developed an absurd courtroom-comedy concept.

This public version removes internal Knowledge Base identifiers and proprietary references.

---

# Original User Request

```text
Let's create another scenario based entirely on your own idea.

You are allowed to develop the concept yourself.

The main requirement is a scenario with many characters and dialogues.
```

---

# Agent Output

## Concept

The agent proposed:

> **“The Pineapple Trial”**

Genre:

**absurd courtroom comedy / ensemble dialogue**

Format:

- vertical 9:16;
- approximately 25–28 seconds;
- cold audience;
- 6 characters.

The central question:

> **What is pineapple actually doing in СОКОчай?**

Instead of providing a long explanation, the product logic is revealed through “witnesses”:

```text
Pineapple
    ↓
Leaf
    ↓
Water
    ↓
Expert
```

The main comedic twist:

**Water arrives late to the courtroom.**

The joke simultaneously helps explain the sequence: the dry product already exists before the brewing stage.

---

# Characters

The agent independently created a six-character ensemble.

### Judge

Treats the absurd case with complete seriousness.

### Prosecutor

Attempts to determine what actually happened.

### Pineapple

Slightly offended that everyone is discussing it.

### Leaf

A calm and extremely concise witness.

### Water

Arrives late carrying a transparent kettle.

### beFruitbe Expert

Does not participate in the argument.

At the end, the Expert places the real dry product and packaging on the table as the key piece of “evidence”.

---

# Hook

## Primary Hook

The first shot:

the Judge hits the gavel.

Pineapple is sitting across from the Judge.

**Judge:**

> Pineapple! What happens to you BEFORE the water appears?

On-screen:

```text
CASE №001
PINEAPPLE × СОКОчай
```

The retention mechanism is the absurdity of expecting an answer from the “accused pineapple”.

---

## Alternative Hook

The door suddenly opens.

Water runs into the room:

> Am I late?!

Everyone:

> YES!

On-screen:

```text
Why is Water the last one here?
```

One of the central moments:

```text

— Am I late?

[short pause]

Everyone:

```

Immediately followed by the key idea:

```text
Because the product already exists before brewing
```

The joke performs two functions:

1. audience retention;
2. product explanation.

# Why AI Was Not Required

An interesting part of the agent's response was that it did **not** recommend using AI generation for the core version.

The main scenes could be produced using:

- actors;
- the real product;
- real packaging;
- the real brewing process.

AI could be added only when it genuinely improved the creative execution.

This demonstrates that the agent did not use generative AI simply because it was available.

The production method was selected according to the communication task.

# Editing Logic

The agent also designed the editing rhythm.

### Opening

Fast pacing:

- new dialogue line;
- reaction;
- quick punch-in

approximately every 1.5–2.5 seconds.

### Transition to Product Proof

After the question:

> So the product already exists before water?

the pace intentionally slows down.

The viewer receives a clean macro shot of the real product.

The transition becomes:

```text
Comedy Sketch
        ↓
Product Evidence
```

# Factuality & Human Verification

Although the user explicitly gave the agent creative freedom, factual product information remained constrained by the Knowledge Base.

Before publication, the agent required verification of:

- the exact product;
- relevant ingredients;
- production-process claims.

The agent also excluded visuals that could create false product information, including:

- unverified ingredients;
- fictional manufacturing scenes;
- modifications of real packaging;
- unsupported representations of the production process.

---

# Series Potential

The agent went beyond a single video and proposed turning the mechanism into a recurring content format.

The Judge and Prosecutor could remain recurring characters while the “case” changes.

For example:

```text
Why does Water arrive last?

What is inside the package BEFORE brewing?

What happens to the product in water?

Who is responsible for the flavour?

Why is the dry product necessary?
```

A single creative concept therefore becomes a potentially repeatable content format.
